# Support

- **Bugs and questions:** open an issue at [github.com/uncoalesced/agent-wire/issues](https://github.com/uncoalesced/agent-wire/issues). Include `wire version`, your OS, your agent client, and the URL or command that misbehaved. `wire status` output helps with sites that fail to load.
- **Security problems:** please don't open a public issue. Report privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). You'll get a reply within 7 days.
- **Email:** mail@uncoalesced.com.
- **Documentation:** [DOCUMENTATION.md](DOCUMENTATION.md) and the [README](README.md).
